## Space Apps demo rules (project submission guide)

- Demo is **either** a video of at most **30 seconds** **or** at most **7 slides** (title slide included).
- Hosted on a public link (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub…); video needs **English subtitles**, slides in **English**.
- Must show the team name, the members, the challenge, and how space agency open data made the project possible.
- Going over 30 s or 7 slides makes the project ineligible for global judging.

## Seven slides (built live in the site: `/#/present`, print to PDF)

1. **Title** — Oman Analog Explorer · Team VEX · members · challenge 10
2. **Challenge** — Moon base locations and Mars landing sites; why analog sites matter
3. **Open NASA data** — 5 Omani sites, 4 Mars regions, 1 lunar south pole region, 8 NASA datasets
4. **Method** — NASA data → raw metrics → normalise (463 m grid) → score + confidence
5. **Results** — Omani sites ranked by mean Mars similarity, each with its closest twin
6. **Can we trust it?** — weight sensitivity, AMADEE-18 check (#4 of 5, explained), measured vs missing, shared-absence rule
7. **Impact and next steps** — mission planners, Oman, open research; field campaign concept; roadmap
